Sponsorship & Financial Transparency

AMEVA 재단 후원 및 재정 투명성 정책

Public Good Commitment

All software created by AOSF is free, open-source, and available to the world without commercial barriers. Sponsorship funds are strictly allocated to mobile test hardware, continuous integration infrastructure, and domain maintenance.

1. Official Support Channels

AOSF accepts contributions through transparent, publicly auditable platforms:

  • GitHub Sponsors: https://github.com/sponsors/uno-km
  • Open Collective: https://opencollective.com/ameva-foundation

2. Sponsor Tiers & Recognition

Tier Level Monthly Contribution Recognition & Community Benefits
Community Supporter $5 / mo Listed in official repository SPONSORS.md directory.
Bronze Sponsor $25 / mo Profile link featured on the official Foundation website.
Silver Sponsor $100 / mo Silver sponsor badge displayed on all foundation project READMEs.
Gold Corporate Sponsor $500 / mo Corporate logo and permanent link featured on main site and TLP repos.
Platinum Partner $2,000+ / mo Quarterly technical advisory sessions and dedicated engineering workshops.

3. Financial Independence Principle

Sponsors do not receive preferential influence over project technical roadmaps, licensing decisions, or code review outcomes. All foundation ledgers and expenditures are published openly on Open Collective.
