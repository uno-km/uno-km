v1.4.6 - Native Vulkan Compute Canny & 5-Backend Standard

Production Release 2026-09-29

  • Galaxy Fleet Multi-Device Validation: Confirmed 100% operational integrity on Galaxy S25 (Adreno 830), Galaxy S20 (Snapdragon 865), and Galaxy A35 (Exynos 1380).
