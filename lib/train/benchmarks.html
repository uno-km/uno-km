3. Training Convergence (MNIST Sanity Check)

# Reproduce: MNIST 2-layer MLP training
python -m termux_train.examples.mnist \
  --epochs 10 --batch-size 32 --lr 0.01

# Results (Galaxy S20, NumPy backend):
# Epoch  1/10 | Loss: 2.3012 | Acc:  9.8% | Time: 48.2s
# Epoch  5/10 | Loss: 0.3821 | Acc: 88.3% | Time: 48.6s
# Epoch 10/10 | Loss: 0.1934 | Acc: 94.2% | Time: 48.1s
# Total training time: 8m 2s
